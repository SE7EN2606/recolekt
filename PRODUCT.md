# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Recolekt is for people who save useful short-form recipe videos from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and similar sources, then need those saves to become a usable personal cookbook. They are often returning later in a practical context: deciding what to cook, reopening a saved recipe, checking ingredients, following steps in the kitchen, adding notes, or cooking a recipe again.

The user wants the app to remember and organize what they saved without making them manage extraction details. Their primary job is not bookmarking content; it is turning scattered recipe videos into meals they can confidently find, cook, adapt, and reuse.

## Product Purpose

Recolekt turns short-form recipe videos into structured, reusable personal knowledge. Recipes are the first vertical, and the current product focus is retention validation through the core loop of saving recipes, organizing them, searching or reopening them, cooking them, adding notes, and returning to cook again.

Success looks like a calm personal cookbook that makes a recipe feel immediately usable. The product should emphasize "I can easily cook and reuse this" rather than "AI extracted this recipe." Extraction, confidence, transcript, hashtags, provenance, and raw metadata should be hidden or collapsed unless the user is explicitly inspecting or editing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, personal, trustworthy.

Recolekt should feel useful, organized, warm, restrained, and consumer-simple, even though the backend and extraction engine are complex. The interface should be familiar enough to trust, personal enough to return to, and focused enough to support real cooking workflows without distraction.

## Anti-references

Recolekt should not look or behave like a generic bookmark manager, social-media feed, gamified app, public profile network, dense enterprise dashboard, or AI inspector/debug interface.

Avoid excessive cards, gradients, animations, badges, metadata, confidence readouts, hashtags, transcripts, and extraction details in normal Library Mode and Cook Mode. The app should not foreground "AI analyzed this" when the user needs "I can use this now."

## Design Principles

1. Make recipes reusable, not merely saved.

2. Prioritize the retention loop: save, organize, retrieve, cook, note, return.

3. Keep Library Mode calm, visual, collection-oriented, and cookbook-like.

4. Keep Cook Mode focused on execution: ingredients, current step, progress, timers, and completion.

5. Move extraction complexity into Inspect/Edit Mode so trust is available without making the main experience feel technical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support reduced motion, color-blind-safe states, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, adequate contrast, and minimum 44 x 44 px mobile touch targets.

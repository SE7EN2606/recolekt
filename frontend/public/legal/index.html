Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

Recolekt is committed to your right to erasure under GDPR and Meta Platform Policy. You can request deletion of your personal data and any Instagram/Facebook data we hold at any time using the methods below.

Meta Platform Requirement: When you remove the Recolekt app from your Facebook or Instagram account settings, Meta automatically sends us a signed deletion request. We process this within 30 days and confirm deletion to you.

Option A – Delete via Recolekt Account Settings

1
Sign in to your Recolekt account at recolekt.app.
2
Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
3
Click "Delete My Account & All Data". This permanently removes all your data from our systems, including any Instagram metadata we have processed on your behalf.
4
You will receive a confirmation email at your registered address within 24 hours.

Option B – Revoke Instagram / Facebook Access

1
Go to Facebook Settings → Security and Login → Apps and Websites, or visit facebook.com/settings?tab=applications.
2
Find Recolekt and click Remove.
3
Meta will automatically notify Recolekt. All Instagram/Facebook data linked to your account will be deleted within 30 days.
4
You can check the status of your deletion request by emailing us at info@recolekt.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will provide a confirmation code and status URL.

Option C – Email Request

Send a deletion request to info@recolekt.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include your registered email address. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email)
  • All Reel metadata and library content you have saved
  • AI-generated summaries, transcriptions, and ingredient data tied to your account
  • Any Instagram/Facebook data (username, profile picture, media metadata) obtained via Meta's Graph API
  • Subscription and billing history (except records required by law)

Confirmation

After deletion, you will receive a confirmation email containing a unique confirmation code and a status URL to verify the deletion was completed. This is required by Meta's Platform Policy.
